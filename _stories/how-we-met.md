## How We Met

<!-- Replace this with your own story! -->

It started with a chance encounter at a mutual friend's birthday party in 2019.
Alex was the one laughing loudest in the corner; Jordan was the one who
couldn't stop glancing over. A conversation about terrible movies turned into
two hours of talking, a traded phone number, and a first date neither of them
expected would change everything.

What followed was the kind of slow-burn that sneaks up on you — coffee
turning into dinner, dinner turning into long walks, long walks turning into
the realization that you've found the person you want next to you for all
of life's adventures.
